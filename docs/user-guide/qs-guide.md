# User Guide - QS Professionals

## 🎯 Welcome to AI QS Assistant!

This guide will help you use the AI assistant to get instant answers about construction pricing.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Sign Up (30 seconds)

1. Go to the registration page
2. Select **"I'm a QS Professional"**
3. Choose **"Create new company"** or **"Join existing company"**
4. Enter your email, password, and name
5. Click **"Create account"**
6. ✅ **You're in!** No approvals needed.

### Step 2: Start Asking Questions

1. Go to the **Chat** page
2. Type your question in the input box
3. Press Enter or click Send
4. Get instant answer with real supplier prices!

---

## 💬 How to Ask Questions

### Price Queries

**Examples:**
- "What's the price of cement?"
- "How much does steel cost?"
- "What's the current price for sand?"

**What you get:**
- All supplier prices for that product
- Best price highlighted
- Price per unit clearly shown

### Cost Calculations

**Examples:**
- "How much for 100 bags of cement?"
- "What's the total cost for 50 steel bars and 200kg of sand?"
- "Calculate cost for 1000 sq ft flooring"

**What you get:**
- Breakdown by product
- Best prices used
- Total cost calculated
- Step-by-step calculation

### Specification Questions

**Examples:**
- "What's the difference between M20 and M25 concrete?"
- "What are the properties of cement?"
- "What's the standard size for steel bars?"

**What you get:**
- Detailed specifications
- Current prices (if available)
- Practical recommendations

### General Questions

**Examples:**
- "What materials do I need for a foundation?"
- "How do I calculate concrete quantity?"
- "What's the best practice for material estimation?"

**What you get:**
- Expert answers
- Construction best practices
- Helpful tips

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Issue: No supplier prices shown

**Possible reasons:**
- No suppliers have added that product yet
- Product name doesn't match exactly

**Solution:**
- Try different product names
- Ask suppliers to add the product

### Issue: Calculation seems wrong

**Solution:**
- Check the quantities you specified
- Verify product names are correct
- Ask for clarification: "Can you show the breakdown?"

### Issue: Slow response

**Solution:**
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Wait a moment and try again
