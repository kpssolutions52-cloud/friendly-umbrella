# Quick Start Guide - QS AI Agent

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Steps

### For QS Professionals

#### Step 1: Sign Up (30 seconds)

1. Go to registration page
2. Select **"I'm a QS Professional"**
3. Choose **"Create new company"** or **"Join existing company"**
4. Enter:
   - Email
   - Password
   - Your name (optional)
5. Click **"Create Account"**
6. ✅ **You're in!** No approvals needed.

#### Step 2: Ask Your First Question (10 seconds)

1. Go to **AI Chat** page
2. Type: **"What's the price of cement?"**
3. Press Enter
4. ✅ **Get instant answer with real supplier prices!**

#### Step 3: Create Your First Project (30 seconds)

1. In chat, type: **"Create a new project for office building"**
2. AI Agent creates the project
3. Add materials: **"I need 100 bags of cement and 50 steel bars"**
4. Generate quote: **"Generate quote for this project"**
5. ✅ **Get complete quote instantly!**

**Total time: < 2 minutes**  
**Time saved: 2-3 hours of phone calls**

---

### For Suppliers

#### Step 1: Sign Up (30 seconds)

1. Go to registration page
2. Select **"I'm a Supplier"**
3. Choose **"Create new supplier"** or **"Join existing supplier"**
4. Enter:
   - Email
   - Password
   - Your name (optional)
5. Click **"Create Account"**
6. ✅ **You're in!** No approvals needed.

#### Step 2: Add Your First Product (1 minute)

1. Go to **Products** page
2. Click **"Add Product"**
3. Fill in:
   - Product Name: **"Cement"**
   - Price: **50.00**
   - Unit: **"bag"**
4. Click **"Save"**
5. ✅ **Your product now appears in AI answers!**

#### Step 3: Update Price (30 seconds)

1. Click on your product
2. Change price: **50.00 → 48.00**
3. Click **"Save"**
4. ✅ **New price appears in AI answers within 30 seconds!**

**Total time: < 2 minutes**  
**Result: Your prices visible to all QS professionals**
