### 3. Transaction Fees

**Model:**
- **2-5% commission** on orders placed through platform
- **Average Order Value:** $5,000-$50,000 per order
- **Commission per Order:** $100-$2,500

**Rationale:**
- **Benchmark:** 
  - Amazon Marketplace: 6-15% commission
  - Etsy: 6.5% transaction fee
  - Construction marketplaces: 3-8% commission
- **Our Rate:** 2-5% (competitive, supplier-friendly)
- **Value:** Platform provides customer acquisition, payment processing, order management

**Transaction Volume Assumptions:**
- **Year 1:** 10% of users place orders monthly

## 📊 Key Metrics & Assumptions

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Assumptions:**
- **QS CAC:** $500-$1,000 per user
  - Source: B2B SaaS benchmarks ($500-$2,000)
  - Our target: $750 average
- **Supplier CAC:** $1,000-$2,000 per supplier
  - Source: B2B SaaS benchmarks ($1,000-$5,000)
  - Our target: $1,500 average

**Year 1 CAC:**
- QS: 1,000 users × $750 = $750K
- Suppliers: 500 suppliers × $1,500 = $750K
- **Total CAC: $1.5M**

**Year 2 CAC:**
- QS: 10,000 users × $750 = $7.5M
- Suppliers: 5,000 suppliers × $1,500 = $7.5M
- **Total CAC: $15M**

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Assumptions:**
- **Average Customer Lifetime:** 3 years (36 months)
- **Monthly Churn Rate:** 3-5% (industry average: 5-7% for B2B SaaS)
- **Our Target:** 4% monthly churn = 25 months average lifetime

**LTV Calculation:**
- **QS Professional ($199/month average):**
  - LTV = $199 × 25 months = **$4,975**
  - LTV:CAC Ratio = $4,975 / $750 = **6.6:1** (healthy: >3:1)
- **Supplier ($299/month average):**
  - LTV = $299 × 25 months = **$7,475**
  - LTV:CAC Ratio = $7,475 / $1,500 = **5:1** (healthy: >3:1)

### Churn Rate Assumptions

**Monthly Churn:**
- **Year 1:** 5% (early stage, product-market fit)
- **Year 2:** 4% (improving retention)
- **Year 3:** 3% (mature product, strong retention)

**Annual Churn:**
- **Year 1:** ~46% (1 - 0.95^12)
- **Year 2:** ~39% (1 - 0.96^12)
- **Year 3:** ~31% (1 - 0.97^12)

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- B2B SaaS average: 5-7% monthly churn
- Our target: 3-5% (better than average)

### Conversion Rates

**Free Trial to Paid:**
- **QS:** 30% (industry average: 25-40%)
- **Suppliers:** 25% (industry average: 20-35%)

**Trial Duration:**
- **QS:** 14-day free trial
- **Suppliers:** 30-day free trial

### Unit Economics

**Year 1 Unit Economics:**

**QS Professional:**
- CAC: $750
- Monthly Revenue: $199
- Gross Margin: 80% (SaaS typical)
- Monthly Contribution Margin: $199 × 80% = $159.20
- **Payback Period:** $750 / $159.20 = **4.7 months** (healthy: <12 months)
- **LTV:** $4,975
- **LTV:CAC:** 6.6:1

**Supplier:**
- CAC: $1,500
- Monthly Revenue: $299
- Gross Margin: 80%
- Monthly Contribution Margin: $299 × 80% = $239.20
- **Payback Period:** $1,500 / $239.20 = **6.3 months** (healthy: <12 months)
- **LTV:** $7,475
- **LTV:CAC:** 5:1

**Both segments show healthy unit economics with strong LTV:CAC ratios and fast payback periods.**
