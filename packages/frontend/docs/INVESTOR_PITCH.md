## 💡 The Problem

### The Construction Industry is Broken

**For Quantity Surveyors (QS):**
- Spend 2-3 hours daily calling suppliers for prices
- Manual quote preparation takes hours
- No real-time price visibility
- Phone tag and follow-ups waste time
- Project management is fragmented

**For Suppliers:**
- Constant phone calls interrupt workflow
- Manual quote preparation is time-consuming
- No visibility into market demand
- Price updates require calling every customer
- Limited reach to potential customers

**For Homeowners/General Users:**
- No idea where to start with construction projects
- Can't get instant quotes
- Don't know fair prices
- No guidance through the process
- Overwhelmed by complexity

**The Cost:**
- **$50+ billion wasted annually** on inefficient construction processes
- **Millions of hours lost** on phone calls and manual work
- **Poor decision-making** due to lack of real-time data
